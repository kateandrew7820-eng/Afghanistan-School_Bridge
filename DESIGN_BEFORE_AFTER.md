## Typography Scale

### BEFORE (Limited scale)
```
Heading:     Some Title            (2xl)
Text:        Regular paragraph     (base)
Small:       small text            (sm)
(Very limited hierarchy)
```

### AFTER (Full responsive scale)
```
h1: LARGE HEADING                    text-4xl lg:text-5xl
h2: Section Heading                  text-3xl lg:text-4xl
h3: Subsection Heading               text-2xl lg:text-3xl
h4: Card Title                       text-xl  lg:text-2xl
h5: Subheading                       text-lg
h6: Small Heading                    text-base
body: Regular paragraph text         text-base + letter-spacing
small: Auxiliary text               text-sm
(Full responsive scale with proper hierarchy)
```

**Improvements:**
- ✅ Clear visual hierarchy (h1-h6)
- ✅ Responsive sizing (changes at lg breakpoint)
- ✅ Proper letter-spacing for readability
- ✅ Font weights (300, 400, 600, 700)
- ✅ Line heights optimized per size
